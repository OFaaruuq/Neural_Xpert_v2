Cookie Policy

Neural Xpert uses cookies and similar technologies on neuralxpert.com to keep the site working, remember basic preferences, and understand which pages are used.

Essential cookies support page loading, security, and form submissions. A first-party cookie named nx_visitor counts page views and return visits for the internal team. It does not store your name or email. Each page view also records the IP address, browser type, and page path so staff can review site traffic. We do not use cookies to sell personal information.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some site features may not work if essential cookies are disabled. Questions can be sent to partnerships@neuralxpert.com.
